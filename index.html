Info

Saisei Builder is a labor of love. It started out as a simple idea to make something that could help people make and share their builds, and slowly turned into something much bigger than I originally expected.

A lot of time has gone into this project. There have been plenty of things to fix, rewrite, rethink, and occasionally completely redo. Some parts probably took much longer than they should have, but I wanted the builder to feel like something that was actually made with care rather than just thrown together.

A lot of the small things you see here exist because someone gave feedback, pointed something out, suggested an idea, made an asset, or just took the time to use the site and tell me what they thought. I really appreciate everyone who has helped along the way, whether their contribution was big or small.

Saisei Builder is still a work in progress, and there is a lot more I want to add and improve. But I am proud of how far it has come.

Thank you for using it, and thank you to everyone who has helped make it what it is.

Made by Mari, The Western Archivist.
